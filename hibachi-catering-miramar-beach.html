Frequently Asked Questions - Miramar Beach Hibachi

Do you travel to Miramar Beach?

Yes! We serve all of Miramar Beach and the surrounding areas including Silver Sands, Sandestin, Grand Boulevard, Seascape. We bring everything to your location.

What do I need to provide?

Nothing! We bring the grill, propane, ingredients, plates, utensils, and sauces. For table and chair setup, we can arrange your venue's furniture for an additional $10/person (it's heavy work!). You just need a flat area of about 10x10 feet (patio, driveway, pool deck, or backyard).

Can you set up at my vacation rental?

Absolutely! Vacation rentals and Airbnbs are our most popular venue type in Miramar Beach. We set up outside and handle all cleanup, so there's no mess or damage to the property.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend 1-2 weeks advance booking, especially for weekends during peak season (March-October). Weekday events can sometimes be booked on shorter notice.

What happens if it rains?

We cook rain or shine! We just need a covered area like a garage, covered patio, or tent. We'll coordinate with you before the event.
