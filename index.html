FAQ

Common Questions

What do I need to provide?

We bring the grill, propane, plates, utensils, napkins, all ingredients, sauces, and condiments. You just provide the guests and the backyard.

What's the minimum order?

There's a $500 minimum per event. $50/person (2 proteins), $60/person (3 proteins), $35/person Buffet style, $30/child (1 protein). Every meal includes salad, vegetables, and fried rice.

How much space do you need?

We need roughly a 10x10 ft flat area for the grill setup. A patio, driveway, pool deck, or backyard all work perfectly. We can set up your venue's tables & chairs for an additional $10/person (setup & carrying fee).

What if it rains?

We cook rain or shine! We just need a covered area - a garage, patio cover, tent, or large covered porch works great. We'll coordinate with you ahead of time.

How far in advance should I book?

Weekends fill up fast, especially during peak season (March-October). We recommend booking 1-2 weeks in advance. Weekday events are easier to accommodate on short notice.

Do you serve at vacation rentals and Airbnbs?

Absolutely! This is one of our most popular requests. We serve at beach houses, vacation rentals, Airbnbs, and VRBOs all along the Florida coast. We handle everything so there's no damage or mess.
