Hibachi Catering at Your Vacation Rental

Updated July 2026 · 5 min read

If you're staying at a beach house, Airbnb, or VRBO in Florida, booking a private hibachi chef is the single best thing you can do to upgrade your vacation. It's consistently rated the #1 activity by our guests, and once you experience it, you'll understand why.

Why Hibachi at a Vacation Rental?

Think about it: you're at a beautiful beach house with friends or family. Everyone's had a long day at the beach. Instead of piling into cars, driving to a crowded restaurant, waiting for a table, and driving back — a professional hibachi chef shows up at YOUR rental with everything. Fresh food, fire show, tables, chairs, and complete cleanup. You don't lift a finger.

It combines dinner, entertainment, and bonding all in one experience, right where you're staying.

Will It Damage the Rental?

Absolutely not. This is one of the most common concerns, and we've done hundreds of events at vacation rentals without a single issue. Here's why:

  • We set up outside on the patio, deck, driveway, or yard
  • We can set up your rental's tables & chairs for you (+$10/person)
  • All cooking happens on our portable grill — nothing touches the property
  • We handle 100% of cleanup — pack up everything and leave the space spotless
  • No grease, no stains, no mess left behind

When to Book During Your Trip

Most guests book hibachi for their first or second night as the "welcome dinner" for the group. Others save it for the last night as the grand finale. Either way works great — just make sure to book 1-2 weeks before your trip to guarantee your preferred date.

Perfect for Large Groups

Vacation rentals often have 15-30+ guests. Our hibachi service scales perfectly for these groups:

  • 10-15 guests: Full interactive hibachi show experience
  • 15-30 guests: Hibachi show + additional grill station
  • 30+ guests: Buffet style at $35/person — best value for large groups
