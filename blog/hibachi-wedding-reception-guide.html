Hibachi Catering for Your Wedding

Updated July 2026 · 5 min read

More Florida couples are discovering that hibachi catering creates one of the most memorable wedding dining experiences possible. Whether it's a rehearsal dinner for 20 or a reception for 100+, the combination of live entertainment and fresh-cooked food makes hibachi a standout choice.

Why Hibachi for Weddings?

  • Built-in entertainment — the fire show replaces the need for separate entertainment
  • Interactive dining — guests watch their food being cooked, creating natural conversation
  • Fresh and impressive — everything cooked to order, not sitting in a warming tray
  • Instagram-worthy — towering flames and knife tricks create incredible photo opportunities
  • All-inclusive — food, show, setup, cleanup included. Table/chair setup available
  • Unique and memorable — your guests will talk about it for years

Best Wedding Events for Hibachi

Rehearsal Dinner

The rehearsal dinner is the most popular wedding event for hibachi. Typically 15-30 guests, the intimate size is perfect for the interactive hibachi show. Everyone sits together around the grill, creating a warm and connected atmosphere the night before the big day.

Welcome Party

Hosting a welcome party for out-of-town guests? Hibachi at a beach house or rental creates an incredible first impression and sets the tone for the wedding weekend.

Reception

For receptions of 30+ guests, our buffet style service at $35/person is the most popular choice. We set up professional stations and keep food flowing all evening. For smaller receptions, the full interactive hibachi experience at $50-$60/person is a showstopper.

After-Party

Late-night hibachi after the reception? Yes, it's a thing, and it's amazing. Guests who've been dancing all night love a hot, fresh meal to cap off the celebration.

Wedding Hibachi Pricing

  • Medium (20-40 guests): $50/person or $35/person buffet — $600-$2,000

  • Book 2-4 weeks ahead for wedding dates
