When a Restaurant Makes Sense

  • Small group (2-4 people) — private chef has a $500 minimum
  • Spontaneous dinner — no advance booking needed
  • You want the restaurant atmosphere — some people prefer dining out

When a Private Chef Wins

  • Groups of 10+ — significantly cheaper per person
  • Special occasions — birthdays, anniversaries, celebrations
  • Vacation rentals — the ultimate beach house upgrade
  • Kids are involved — they can run around freely
  • Convenience — no driving, no waiting, no parking
  • BYOB — bring your own drinks and save hundreds
  • Privacy — no sharing the experience with strangers
  • Better show — the chef performs exclusively for your group

The Hidden Costs of Restaurants

People often think restaurants are cheaper, but they forget to factor in the full cost:

  • Tax (7% in Florida)
  • Mandatory 18-20% tip for large parties
  • Drinks at $8-$15 each
  • Gas and parking
  • Appetizers and desserts
  • Time spent waiting (your vacation time has value!)

When you add it all up, a restaurant dinner for 10 people can easily cost $800-$1,200+. A private hibachi chef for the same group? $550.

The Verdict

For groups of 10 or more, a private hibachi chef is better value, more convenient, more private, and more fun than a restaurant. The only reason to choose a restaurant is for very small groups or completely spontaneous dinners.
