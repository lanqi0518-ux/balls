Hibachi at Home: The Complete Guide

Updated July 2026 · 6 min read

Hosting a hibachi party at home is one of the best ways to entertain guests. Whether you're celebrating a birthday, hosting friends at your vacation rental, or just want a memorable dinner party, a private hibachi chef brings the full Japanese teppanyaki experience to your backyard, patio, or pool deck.

What Is Hibachi at Home?

Hibachi at home (also called "backyard hibachi" or "private hibachi") is a catering service where a professional teppanyaki chef comes to your location with everything needed to cook and perform a live hibachi show. Think of it as bringing the Japanese steakhouse experience to your backyard — but better, because it's private, customized, and you don't have to drive anywhere.

What Does the Chef Bring?

A professional hibachi catering service brings absolutely everything. You literally need to provide nothing except guests and a flat surface. Here's what arrives with the chef:

  • Professional flat-top teppanyaki grill with propane tank
  • Fresh premium ingredients — proteins, rice, vegetables, sauces
  • Table & chair setup available using your venue's furniture (+$10/person)
  • Tablecloths and decorations
  • Plates, utensils, and napkins
  • Squeeze bottles for sauce tricks
  • Fire show equipment — oil for flames, onion for volcano

Space Requirements

You need a flat area of approximately 10x10 feet for the grill setup. This can be:

  • A backyard or lawn
  • A patio or pool deck
  • A driveway or garage (great for rainy days)
  • A covered pavilion or tent
  • A vacation rental deck or balcony area

The chef positions the grill so all guests can sit around it and watch the show. For groups larger than 10-12, the chef will set up additional serving areas.

How the Evening Works

  1. Chef arrives 45 minutes early — sets up grill, arranges your venue's tables & chairs, and preps all ingredients
  2. Guests gather around the grill — the show begins with the fire performance
  3. Live cooking for 60-90 minutes — includes onion volcano, flying shrimp tricks, flame towers, and fresh cooking
  4. Everyone eats together — food is served hot directly from the grill
  5. Chef handles complete cleanup — packs everything up, leaves your space spotless

Best Locations for Hibachi at Home

Backyard

The classic setup. Your backyard provides privacy, plenty of space, and the comfort of home. Perfect for birthday parties, family gatherings, and casual celebrations.

Pool Deck / Patio

Watching a fire show next to the pool is an incredible atmosphere. The flat surface of a pool deck is ideal for grill placement.

Beach House / Vacation Rental

This is our #1 most popular venue type. Hibachi at a vacation rental turns a regular beach trip into an unforgettable experience. We handle everything and leave no mess.

Garage / Covered Area

Rainy day? No problem. A garage or covered patio provides shelter while still allowing the fire show to happen safely.

Tips for the Perfect Hibachi Party

  • Book 1-2 weeks ahead — weekends fill up fast, especially in summer
  • Tell us about allergies — we accommodate all dietary restrictions
  • Have drinks ready — we bring the food, you handle beverages
  • Invite neighbors — the fire show draws attention, might as well include them!
  • Take photos and video — the fire tricks make incredible content
  • Let kids sit up front — they love the onion volcano and flying shrimp
