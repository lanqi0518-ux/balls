How Does This Compare to a Hibachi Restaurant?

Going to a hibachi restaurant in the Destin or 30A area typically costs $30-$50 per person for food alone — before tax, tip, drinks, and travel. When you factor in everything, a restaurant dinner for 10 people can easily cost $600-$800+.

With private hibachi catering at $50/person, you get the same quality food, a better fire show (we perform in YOUR space), and the convenience of not having to drive anywhere. Plus, everything is included — no tipping required (though always appreciated!).

Tips to Save Money on Hibachi Catering

  1. Book weekdays — more availability and sometimes special rates
  2. Choose the 2-protein package ($50) instead of 3-protein ($65)
  3. Use buffet style for large groups — $35/person saves significantly at 30+ guests
  4. Kids menu is $30/child — don't pay adult prices for children
  5. Book early — last-minute bookings may have limited availability
